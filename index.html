The main button saves the PDF and opens a new email — attach the saved PDF before sending. "Share" hands the PDF file directly to an app of your choice.

How to Send on iPhone (Outlook)

1. Tap Generate PDF & Email via Outlook above.
2. The PDF downloads — tap Download at the bottom of Safari, then tap the file to confirm it saved.
3. Switch to the Outlook app (or the mail app that opens automatically) and start a new email, or continue in the email window that opened.
4. Tap the paperclip / attach icon in the email, choose Browse Files or Recent, and select the timesheet PDF you just saved.
5. Fill in the recipient, check the attachment is there, and hit Send.
Tip: tap Or Share PDF to an App Instead to skip the download step — pick Outlook straight from the share sheet and the PDF attaches automatically; you'll just need to add the recipient yourself.
